v3 — Financial Health Scorecard shipped

The differentiator. India has no good free tool for "am I doing well with my money?" — this fills the gap. Builds on top of v1 tracker data so we don't ask users to type "current monthly expenses" by hand.

5-pillar score (each 0–20, total 100):

Emergency fund — months of expenses in liquid savings6 months stable jobs · 9–12 freelancers
Insurance — term cover ÷ (10× annual income) + health cover adequacy10–15× income if dependents · ₹5L individual + ₹15L super top-up · push back on "everyone needs term"
Debt — EMI ÷ income · CC carry-over · debt mixEMI < 40% · no rolling CC balance · prefer secured
Savings rate — (income − expenses) ÷ income20%+ good · 30%+ excellent
Investing — net worth growth · equity allocation vs age · retirement target (NPS/PPF/EPF/MFs)Equity % ≈ 100 − age · retirement target ~25× annual expenses

Onboarding wizard (5 questions): monthly income · current liquid savings · term life cover · health cover · total monthly EMIs. Drill-down per pillar adds NPS, PPF, gold, MFs etc.

Disclaimers (locked, must appear): "Rules of thumb, not financial advice. We are not a SEBI-registered investment advisor." Every metric has a "why" tooltip linking to a short explainer.

Out of scope for v3: peer comparison · insurance/investment product recommendations + affiliates · auto-pull from EPFO/NPS/banks. Effort: ~8–12h for v3 (wizard + scoring + drill-down). v4 score history + goals: ~4h.
